## Evidence and notices

Historical QA notes, logs and screenshots are retained locally and are not part of the public repository. Current evidence belongs in [Validation](VALIDATION.md). License texts and generated third-party notices remain in their original language and are not translated.

## Maintaining the three versions

Update the English page and both matching translations together. Keep numbers, commands, table rows, scope and citations equivalent; translate link labels, but never identifiers, asset filenames or original license text. Navigation within a language must stay in that language. Use matching topic filenames under `docs/en/`, `docs/vi/` and `docs/zh-CN/`. Record new verification in VALIDATION; retain superseded QA notes locally.
